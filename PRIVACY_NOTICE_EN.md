## 3. Purposes of processing

**Necessary purposes.** These give rise to and support our relationship with you, so they do not require additional consent:

a) Reviewing your demo request and deciding whether to approve it.
b) Creating, managing, authenticating and protecting your account, including managing the 7-day demo access period and its expiration.
c) Saving your preferences so the platform works the way you set it up.
d) Sending you service communications: credentials, confirmations, changes to your access status and expiration notices.
e) Preventing abuse and fraud (for example, rejecting temporary email addresses) and keeping the service secure.
f) Handling your requests, including ARCO rights requests, and complying with legal obligations.

**Optional purposes.** These require your consent:

g) Sending you our newsletter and editorial content on private capital in Latin America.
h) Analyzing your feedback survey responses to improve the product.
i) Contacting you about plans, launches or a possible commercial relationship after your demo.

Declining the optional purposes does not affect your access to DFP. You can decline by leaving the corresponding boxes unchecked when you sign up, and at any time afterward through the options in section 7.

If we ever intend to make any other transfer, we will update this notice and obtain your consent where the law requires it.

## 6. Cookies and browser storage

DFP does not use its own cookies or any advertising, analytics or session-recording tools. We use your browser's local storage (localStorage) only for:

| Key | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `sb-*` | Keeping you securely signed in. |
| `dfp_market_favorites` | Remembering your favorite markets. |
| `dfp_layout_*` | Remembering your panel layout. |

These keys are deleted when you sign out. Your favorites and panel layout are also saved to your account so they can be restored the next time you sign in. You can clear local storage from your browser settings; if you do, you will need to sign in again.

## 8. ARCO rights

You have the right to **access** your personal data and learn how we process it; to **rectify** it if it is inaccurate, incomplete or out of date; to **cancel** it, meaning to have us delete it from our records; and to **oppose** its processing on legitimate grounds.

**How to exercise them.** Send an email to [PRIVACY EMAIL] with the subject line "ARCO Request" that includes:

1. Your name and an email address or other means to receive our response.
2. A copy of a valid official ID (you may cover any details not needed to identify you) or, if a representative is acting for you, documents proving their identity and authority.
3. A clear description of the data concerned, unless you are requesting access.
4. The right you want to exercise or what you are requesting. If you are asking for a correction, state the change and, where applicable, include a supporting document.
5. Any other information that helps us locate your data.

**Timeframes.** We will send you our response within 20 business days of receiving your request and, if the request is granted, we will carry it out within the following 15 business days. These timeframes may be extended once for an equal period when justified, and we will let you know.

**Cost and delivery.** Exercising ARCO rights is free of charge. We will deliver information by email in digital format.

**Cancellation.** When your data is cancelled, we may keep it blocked solely to address liabilities arising from its processing, for the period set by law, after which it will be deleted.

If you do not receive a response or disagree with it, you may file a rights protection request with Mexico's Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno (Ministry of Anti-Corruption and Good Governance) within 15 business days after we communicate our response.

## 9. Withdrawing consent

You may withdraw any consent you have given us at any time, through the same channel and procedure described in section 8. Withdrawal does not have retroactive effect. If you withdraw consent for necessary purposes, we may be unable to continue providing you access to the platform.

If you appear in it and wish to exercise your ARCO rights, write to [PRIVACY EMAIL] following the procedure in section 8.

If a security breach significantly affects your property or moral rights, we will inform you immediately so you can take appropriate measures to protect them.
